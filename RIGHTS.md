# Rights and attribution

The source report describes a group academic project. This repository reorganizes the supplied technical material into documentation, manually transcribed configuration references and original extracted evidence images. Personal identities and student identifiers are excluded from the published files; the unmodified source documents remain local.

No blanket open-source license is assigned because ownership and redistribution rights for every part of the group report and its images have not been established. Repository access does not grant a new license to third-party software or source material. Obtain the relevant contributors' permission and confirm attribution before public distribution or choosing a license.

Cowrie, Dionaea, Filebeat, Logstash, Elasticsearch, Kibana, Docker, VMware and other named products retain their respective rights and license terms. No third-party program source code or binaries are vendored here. Screenshots preserve the supplied lab evidence; their origin is recorded in the evidence manifest.
